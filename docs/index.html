How it works

One messy sentence in. A whole campaign out.

Real people type "need bf content for ig, 30 percent off". Quint AI understands it, says what it assumed, asks at most three questions and keeps working while you answer.

01 · Understand

Your words, your places

Platforms like "ig, tik tok, status", places like "Lagos", "the Gulf" or "West Africa", offers like "N5k off" and deadlines like "ends nov 30".

02 · Find the moments

Dates that matter to you

60+ curated events and official holidays for 100+ countries, ranked for your audience and niche. A Lagos sneaker shop gets Black Friday and Boxing Day, not Diwali.

03 · Plan

Every post has a job

Tease, warm up, early access, launch, last chance, thank you. Launch days always post. Email and WhatsApp stay capped so people do not unsubscribe.

04 · Create

Fits what you sell

15 niche profiles drive the angles, hooks and shot lists: outsole close-ups for sneakers, fabric in motion for dresses, headlight sweeps for cars.

05 · Stay current

What is happening today

Today's top searches per country, upcoming public holidays and what the world is reading, with sensitive topics flagged so your brand never newsjacks a tragedy.

06 · Sell

A page built around your product

Drop in photos, a phone turntable video or a 3D model. The page is art-directed from what you sell, with motion that follows the scroll.

The calendar

Every month has a reason to sell.

Computed by rule (Easter, Thanksgiving, Mother's Day) or verified tables (Ramadan, Eid, Lunar New Year, Diwali, Hanukkah), with a note where moon sighting can move a date. Plus live official holidays for 100+ countries.

Highlighted: high-impact shopping moments. This strip is generated from the same data the tools use.

Content kits

Finished posts, ready to share.

Ask for a kit and get every post for your sale on one page: designed graphics at the right size for each app, with captions written for Instagram, WhatsApp, X and LinkedIn. Open it on your phone, tap Share, and the image goes straight into Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp.

Eight posts per saleOffer post, product spotlights, a carousel, Story and WhatsApp Status, a last-call story and a card for X and LinkedIn.
Captions per appWritten from your real prices and dates. X posts always fit 280 characters. No hook is used twice in a plan.
Your real productsBefore making anything, your assistant asks once: send your photos, use a representative photo (labelled on the post), create images, or go without. Products are never drawn or faked.
Studio photos, freePhone photos are cleaned up on your own computer: colour, sharpness and background removed, placed on a studio colour or a scene of your choice.
Change anything laterAsk for a new photo or caption and the kit updates at the same link.

Honest by default

Built to sell without lying.

What Quint AI never does

  • Invent reviews, results or stock counts
  • Show a "was" price you never charged
  • Run a countdown that resets
  • Use another brand's logo photos (filtered out)
  • Show a stock or drawn picture as your product
  • Sell on remembrance days or around tragedies

What every brief includes

  • Ad and affiliate disclosure
  • AI-image labels where platforms require them
  • Licensed music reminders
  • Niche rules: APR for car finance, no cure claims for beauty, allergens for food
  • Photo credits on every page
  • A label on any representative photo
