## Compliance checks applied (from `asset_brief`)

- Offer terms stated: 30% off, all sneakers, 24 hours, start time with time zones, online only.
- No "was" price shown except real current prices.
- No fake scarcity, no countdown that resets.
- Alt text on images, captions on video, commercial music library.

## Findings from this test

| What | Result |
|---|---|
| Dates and lead times | Correct, no checking needed |
| Plan structure (phases) | Good: tease → warm-up → early access → launch |
| Specs and safe zones | Correct and useful |
| Compliance checklist | Useful, caught time zone and music licensing |
| Email cadence | **Too high.** Same 3x/week as social |
| Angles | **Not niche-aware.** "Digital products and courses" suggested for a sneaker brand |
| Hook formulas | **Generic.** The model had to write better hooks itself |
| Irrelevant events | Diwali/Dhanteras shown for a US/NG audience |
| Time for week 1 (plan for the month + 8 drafted pieces) | Under 1 minute of wall time (09:44:08 to 09:45:02). Filming, photos and design still take human time. |
